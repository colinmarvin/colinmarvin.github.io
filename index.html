I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Department of Earth, Environmental, and Planetary Sciences at Brown University. My research sits at the intersection of geomorphology, planetary science, and sedimentology, using aeolian dunes and sand surface textures as windows into the environmental conditions of Earth and other planetary bodies. I completed my Ph.D. in Geological Sciences at Stanford University in 2026 and my B.S. in Geography (Honors) at Arizona State University in 2021.
